## Corrected diagnosis: "resume from history worked 2-3 times, then started from 0"
Owner uses ad blockers: no ads. Wrong call: ads blamed. Likely cause, fixed by _resumeSeek + save hold in the WIP:
1. Race: old code gave up if video.played.length > 0 or currentTime > 3. YouTube autoplays sooner on warm loads (2nd, 3rd visit), so the seek was skipped.
2. Cascade: after one failed resume the video plays from 0 and saves overwrite the saved position (local and cloud, newer wins), so later resumes go near 0.
Verify on device; if it still fails, use "Check this page" right after a history click.
